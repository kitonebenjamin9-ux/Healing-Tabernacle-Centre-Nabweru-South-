How We Began

Founded on Faith, Built on Community

Healing Tabernacle started in 2009 as a small prayer gathering of twelve families in a rented hall. Today we're a growing church family committed to the same mission: seeing broken lives made whole through the power of the Gospel.

From weekly worship services to community outreach, youth mentorship, and prayer ministry, everything we do points back to one truth — healing is available to everyone who calls on the name of Jesus.
